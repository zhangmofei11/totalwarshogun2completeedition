FACTION GUIDE

Choose your clan and conquer Japan

🏴

Shimazu

Difficulty: Easy

Located in Kyushu, the Shimazu clan excels in ashigaru (foot soldier) infantry. Their unique firing drilled units are devastating in prolonged engagements.

Specialty: Ashigaru infantry, firing drill
🐴

Takeda

Difficulty: Medium

Masters of cavalry warfare, the Takeda clan specializes in mounted units. Their fire cavalry and charged cavalry are unmatched on open battlefields.

Specialty: Cavalry, cavalry charge
🔥

Oda

Difficulty: Hard

Starting in Owari with a small territory, the Oda clan relies on superior firepower and aggressive expansion. Early game pressure is critical for success.

Specialty: Firearms, aggressive play
❄️

Uesugi

Difficulty: Medium

The Uesugi excel in diplomatic relations and naval power. Their unique recruitment buildings provide cost reductions across the empire.

Specialty: Diplomacy, naval
🌊

Hojo

Difficulty: Medium

Defensive specialists from the Kanto region, the Hojo excel in siege warfare and fortress defense. Their gunpowder units are exceptionally accurate.

Specialty: Defense, siege, accuracy
⚡

Date

Difficulty: Hard

The Date clan specializes in flanking tactics and early cavalry deployment. Their gunpowder cavalry provides unique tactical options.

Specialty: Flanking, gunpowder cavalry
🌸

Tokimune

Difficulty: Easy

The Hōjō clan leader Tokimune is known for his well-balanced starting position and strong infantry. Perfect for learning the game's mechanics.

Specialty: Balanced, infantry
🎋

Mori

Difficulty: Medium

Masters of naval warfare, the Mori clan controls the seas around the Seto Inland Sea. Their naval units gain experience faster and have unique fire ship abilities.

Specialty: Naval, fire ships
