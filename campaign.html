CAMPAIGN GUIDE

Conquer Japan province by province

📍 Early Game Strategy

First 20 Turns

  • • Consolidate your starting province
  • • Build up economy before expanding
  • • Recruit 2-3 additional generals
  • • Establish alliances through trade
  • • Research military technologies

Economy Basics

  • • Markets increase income per turn
  • • Farms provide food surplus
  • • Ports boost trade revenue
  • • Balance army maintenance with income

⚔️ Military Expansion

Army Composition

Maintain balanced forces: 60% infantry, 25% ranged, 15% cavalry. Adjust based on faction bonuses.

Siege Warfare

Always bring siege equipment. Battering rams for gates, ladders for walls. Siege engines from range first.

Multi-Front War

Avoid fighting multiple clans simultaneously. Secure alliances or eliminate threats one at a time.

🤝 Diplomacy Tips

Trade Agreements

Trade rights provide steady income and improve relations. Always negotiate resource exchanges.

  • • Food surplus for trade goods
  • • Military access for alliances
  • • Non-aggression pacts buy time

Marriage & Alliances

Use marriage diplomacy to secure long-term alliances. Inherit territories through royal marriages.

  • • Check family tree for marriage candidates
  • • Alliance break after leader death
  • • Trust declines after broken agreements

👑 Victory Conditions

Domination Victory

Capture Kyoto and hold it for 20 turns while controlling 50% of provinces. Most challenging but rewarding.

Economic Victory

Accumulate 100,000 koku in your treasury. Requires careful trade management and minimal military spending.
