BATTLE TACTICS

Win every engagement with superior tactics

📐 Basic Formations

Line Formation

Standard infantry line with ranged units behind. Best for pitched battles on flat terrain.

  • • Infantry in center, cavalry on flanks
  • • Archers behind infantry line
  • • General positioned for morale boost

Hammer & Anvil

Infantry pins enemy while cavalry flanks and charges from behind. Classic devastating combo.

  • • Infantry engages front
  • • Cavalry hidden on flanks
  • • Wait for infantry melee then charge

Fighting Withdrawal

Ranged units fire while moving backward, covered by infantry. Effective for attrition.

  • • Keep infantry between ranged and enemy
  • • Slowly retreat while firing
  • • Protects valuable ranged units

Flanking Maneuver

Cavalry sweeps wide to attack enemy from sides or rear. Multiplier effect on charge bonus.

  • • Send cavalry wide around battlefield
  • • Use terrain for concealment
  • • Time charge for maximum impact

🗺️ Terrain Advantages

Hilltop

+20% accuracy, +charge bonus for downhill charges

Forest

-20% ranged accuracy, hidden units until discovered

River Crossing

-40% movement, -morale for crossing units

Narrow Pass

Forces enemy into tight formation, negate numbers

High Grass

Conceals unit movement, good for ambushes

Fortified Position

+40% defense, ideal for defensive battles

🐴 Cavalry Tactics

Charge Impact

Cavalry deals massive damage on charge but vulnerable in prolonged melee. Use charge, then withdraw.

Pursuit vs. Combat

Use pursuit mode after routing enemies. Keep cavalry in combat mode when facing fresh units.

Anti-Cav Deployment

Naginata samurai and yari blocks counter cavalry effectively. Position before cavalry engagement.

Fire Cavalry

Shoot then move away. Hit ranged units then flee before melee. Harass static targets.

🏰 Siege Battle Guide

1.

Approach Phase

Deploy siege equipment first. Bring multiple ladders and rams. Siege towers for walls.

2.

Breach Phase

Use cannons or fire rockets to suppress defenders. Create breach points with battering rams.

3.

Storm Phase

Send expendable units first through breach. Elite samurai follow to hold capture points.

4.

Secure Phase

Capture all gatehouses and central tower. Protect general for morale. Clear remaining resistance.
